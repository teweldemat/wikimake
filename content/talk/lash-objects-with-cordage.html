Purpose

Discussion for improving the lashing article (tension technique, wrap counts for different jobs, and hafting-specific guidance).

Open questions

  • Should hafting (blade-to-handle) get its own article once stone axes/adzes exist, with this article covering only the general technique?
  • Are 8–12 wraps the right default across cord thicknesses, or should the guidance scale with cord diameter?

Proposed improvements

  • Add a wrap-count table by job (gorge tie, sinker, handle, cross-lashing).
  • Add a diagram panel for frapping turns on a cross-lashing.

Decisions

  • Lashing is a separate article from knots; it links to two half-hitches for the lock rather than re-teaching it.
